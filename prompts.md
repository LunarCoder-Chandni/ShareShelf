# ShareShelf — Common User Prompts

## 1. Borrow & Lend

Prompts for students who want to borrow or lend items temporarily.

- I need to borrow a scientific calculator for my mathematics exam tomorrow.
- Is anyone willing to lend me a laptop charger for two days?
- I want to borrow a lab coat for my practical examination.
- Find students who are lending engineering drawing instruments near me.
- I need a book for my upcoming semester exams. Can someone lend it to me?
- I want to lend my calculator to another student for a small fee.
- I have a spare set of lab equipment that other students can borrow.
- Help me find someone who can lend me a tripod for my college project.
- I need a projector for a college presentation this weekend.
- Show me available items that I can borrow within my budget.

## 2. Student Services

Prompts for students offering or looking for skills and services.

- I need someone to help me design a PowerPoint presentation for my project.
- Find a student who can help me debug my C programming code.
- I am looking for affordable graphic design services for a college event.
- Is anyone available to help me with video editing?
- I need help creating a professional resume.
- Find students who offer tutoring for mathematics and programming.
- I can design posters and social media graphics. How can I offer my services?
- I need someone to help me build a basic website for my college project.
- Show me student services available for less than ₹500.
- I am looking for someone who can help with 3D modelling for an engineering assignment.

## 3. Buying & Selling

Prompts for students who want to buy or sell items permanently.

- I want to buy second-hand engineering textbooks at an affordable price.
- Find used scientific calculators available for sale.
- I want to sell my old semester books to juniors.
- Is anyone selling a second-hand keyboard or mouse?
- I need an affordable laptop stand for my hostel room.
- Help me find used engineering drawing tools for sale.
- I want to sell my project components after completing my semester project.
- Find second-hand college supplies under ₹1,000.
- I want to buy a used cycle from another student.
- Show me affordable electronics and accessories listed by students.

## 4. General Discovery & Filters

Prompts for discovering listings across ShareShelf.

- Show me the newest listings added by students.
- Find items available near my campus.
- Show me listings within my budget of ₹500.
- Find affordable academic supplies for engineering students.
- Show me items available for immediate pickup.
- Find listings that are currently available.
- Show me services offered by students in my college.
- Help me compare the prices of similar listings.
- Find items that I can borrow instead of buying.
- Show me recently added books, electronics, and project supplies.

## 5. Safety & Trust

Prompts related to safer exchanges between students.

- How can I verify that a listing belongs to a genuine student?
- What should I check before borrowing an expensive item?
- How can I arrange a safe handover on campus?
- What should I do if an item is returned damaged?
- How can I report a suspicious listing?
- What precautions should I take before paying for a second-hand item?
- How can I contact a listing owner to clarify the item's condition?
- What should I do if someone does not return a borrowed item?

## 6. Listing Creation Examples

Example prompts from students who want to create listings.

- I want to lend my Casio scientific calculator for ₹20 per day.
- Create a sale listing for my used engineering mathematics textbook priced at ₹150.
- I can offer Python tutoring for ₹300 per session.
- I want to sell my old drawing board in good condition for ₹400.
- I want to lend my spare keyboard for three days.
- I offer poster design services for ₹200 per poster.
- I want to sell my unused project components for ₹350.

---

## Purpose

These prompts represent common student needs and use cases for ShareShelf's three core modules:

1. **Borrow & Lend** — Temporary access to useful items.
2. **Student Services** — Skill-sharing and affordable student services.
3. **Buying & Selling** — Permanent exchange of pre-owned goods.

They can be used as sample prompts, demo scenarios, manual test cases, or starter content for an AI-powered search assistant.

**Note:** Prompts are examples of user requests, not proof that matching listings exist. ShareShelf should display results based on actual available listings and should not invent users, prices, or item availability.
